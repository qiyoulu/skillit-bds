# Skillit Kit BDS v1.6 — release notes
**Last updated:** 2026-10-29
**Status:** pending tag `v1.6`. Follows v1.5 (tag `v1.5`).
**Comparison baseline:** v1.5.

## Summary
The footer becomes an approved Iron layout surface on web and email. Email gains a full section spec: section cards, a mobile contract, spacing above the footer, footer hit areas, and preview, alt-text and font-loading rules. The voice rules gain lists, link text, tone by situation and no negative contractions. Two older rules change: the image-to-text gap and disabled buttons. No token values change.

**Migration required:** No for tokens. Review the footer and the image-to-text gap wherever they are built (see Consumers).

## Changed rules
- **Footer is an Iron layout surface** (`components/footer.md`, `components/email.md`, `LLM-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `rules/layout.md`, `tokens/docs/color.md`, `docs/design-reference.md`, `HUMAN-INSTRUCTIONS.md`). Iron ground, Plaster text, links, mark and icons; Plaster on Iron is 13.7135:1. This ends the "no Iron layout band" rule, which was added in the Oct 1 bulk update without a stated reason. Iron stays out of every other layout surface.
- **Image-to-text gap** (`rules/layout.md`): minimum `--space-4` (16px), equal to the paragraph gap. It was `--space-8` (32px).
- **Between unrelated blocks** (`rules/layout.md`): 64px is for web layouts; email follows `components/email.md` (32px between sections).
- **Disabled buttons** (`components/states.md`): avoid disabling submit and primary buttons. When a control must be disabled, use `aria-disabled="true"`, keep 50% opacity, and say why in nearby text.

## Added
- **Email section cards** (`components/email.md`): one Plaster feature card per section on a Canvas body; 32px padding at every size; heading on Canvas above the card; 472px card, 408px content.
- **Email mobile contract:** the 64px outer margin drops to 16px at 600px and narrower; Text-2x becomes 24px and Text-1.5x 18px; body holds at 16px.
- **Email spacing:** 64px above the footer band (32px mobile); no extra outer padding around the column.
- **Email footer:** 40 to 44px hit areas on links and social icons, 16px between icons, and a one-sentence reason line.
- **Email preview, alt text, color scheme and font loading:** preview text of 90 characters or fewer; descriptive alt text on story photos; `color-scheme: light`; note that most clients ignore web fonts and render weight 450 as 400.
- **Email scan emphasis:** bold action verbs and data points, a few phrases per card, following the voice rule.
- **Link text** (`components/links.md`): describes the destination; never "here".
- **Lists and links, tone by situation, negative contractions** (`voice/brand-voice.md`): lists open with a lead-in and keep items to one or two short sentences; tone shifts with the reader's situation; write "cannot", "do not", "does not".

## Changed copy examples
- `voice/copy-examples.md` and `voice/brand-voice.md`: "can't" and "doesn't" become "cannot" and "does not" in five examples.

## Consumers
- **Website footer** (Squarespace, Webflow, Rails): the BDS now specifies an Iron ground with Plaster text and links; the lockup inherits Plaster. The live site may already match; confirm and update `components/footer.md` if it does not.
- **Anything using a 32px image-to-text gap:** 16px is now the minimum; larger gaps still pass.
- **Disabled buttons:** review forms that disable submit until the form is valid.
- **Email (HubSpot):** the November newsletter template follows this spec. Older templates keep their Iron footer band and 16px side padding until they are rebuilt.
- **Copy:** replace negative contractions in new copy; existing published copy is unchanged.

Deploying changes to consumers requires separate approval.
