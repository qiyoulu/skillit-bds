# Skillit Kit BDS v1.10 — release notes
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23
**Status:** v1.10. Follows v1.9 (tag `v1.9`).
**Comparison baseline:** v1.9.

## Summary
Icons, an app shell, and patterns for the Skillit Hire app: chat threads with Skillit AI and workers, a chat input with a send button, search suggestions, and a mobile tab bar. Icon placement in buttons now follows what the icon means, and arrows are allowed on buttons again. Orange "New" tags, the Hire lockup, and desktop touch targets get looser rules that match what ships.

**Migration required:** No. Every change adds an asset or pattern, or loosens a rule. Checkboxes switch to the exported SVGs, which keep the same look. Nothing that ships today breaks, and no tokens changed.

## Added
- **Icons** (`assets/icons/`, `components/icons.md`): 15 tintable icons for Skillit Hire: users, user-group, cog-6-tooth, archive-box, chat-bubble-left, ellipsis-horizontal, hard-hat, minus, no-symbol, slash-circle, and the circle status family (circle, circle-empty, circle-quarter, circle-half, circle-three-quarter), plus `checkbox-bar.svg`. Inventory counts are updated across the docs.
- **App shell** (`components/navigation.md`): 64px header with the Hire lockup, sidebar, and content padding for Skillit Hire.
- **Indeterminate checkbox** (`components/checkboxes.md`): a partly-selected state for header checkboxes, drawn with `checkbox-bar.svg`.
- **Chat** (`components/chat.md`): Plaster message bubbles on Canvas, 16px corners, 12px padding. The person using the app is on the right and the other person on the left; side, not color, says who sent it. 4px between messages from the same sender, 16px when the sender changes. Skillit AI replies are plain text with no bubble, and Skillit AI has no human name or persona.
- **Chat input** (`components/forms.md`): a pill like the search field, with a 40px Traffic Cone send button inside the right end, a 24px Iron up arrow, and the accessible name "Send". Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line.
- **Search suggestions** (`components/forms.md`): a Plaster panel under the field with results grouped by type, bold names over a detail line, and "See all results for “query”" last.
- **Fields on Plaster** (`components/forms.md`): on a Plaster surface such as an app header, a field is Canvas.
- **Mobile tab bar** (`components/navigation.md`): four one-word tabs with icons, always visible on top-level screens, navigation only, a Traffic Cone dot for unread items.
- **Person names in UI** (`tokens/docs/typography.md`): bold when the name is the title of something you can open, regular inside sentences, and in inboxes bold means unread.

## Changed rules
- **Iron surface** (`tokens/docs/color.md`, `rules/layout.md`, `tokens/docs/logo.md`, `LLM-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `docs/design-reference.md`): Iron is a permitted surface alongside Canvas and Plaster, for dark sections, dark-theme decks and documents, floating menus and tooltips, the footer band, modal backdrops, and photographic overlays. The Plaster logo is allowed on any Iron surface.
- **Checkboxes** (`components/checkboxes.md`): 2px Traffic Cone stroke, rendered from the exported SVGs at 24px (20px box). Hover no longer recolors the box. Checkboxes and focus share one color.
- **Lists and tables** (`components/tables.md`, `rules/layout.md`): tables alternate row surfaces instead of ruling lines, with the rationale and sources; lists that are not tables separate items with space.
- **Button icon placement** (`components/buttons.md`): icons that point where the action goes (forward arrows, external link, dropdown chevron) follow the label. Icons that name the action or its object (add, edit, delete, call, calendar, filter, back) lead it. Icon-only buttons have no side.
- **Arrows on buttons** (`components/buttons.md`, `components/links.md`): the "no arrow on buttons" rule and the standalone-sentence requirement for arrow links are removed.
- **Highlight tags** (`components/badges.md`): one Traffic Cone tag per card is allowed at Text-0.75x or Text-1x, on Plaster or Canvas, as a documented brand exception (3.0177:1 on Plaster, 2.6958:1 on Canvas).
- **Hire lockup** (`tokens/docs/logo.md`, `components/navigation.md`, `components/footer.md`, `LLM-INSTRUCTIONS.md`): use the Hire lockup wherever it fits, 32px high or down to 24px; use the mark where it does not. Replaces "at every width".
- **Touch targets** (`rules/accessibility.md`): dense desktop screens used with a mouse may use 32px buttons and chips (WCAG 2.5.8 is 24px). Anything usable by touch keeps 40px.
- **Card padding** (`components/cards.md`): cards with 16px corners have equal padding on every side. Exceptions: tags and chips keep paired padding, chat bubbles use 12px, dense sidebar rows use 0 / 8px.

## Consumers
- **Rails (Skillit Hire):** use the chat, chat input, search suggestions, and tab bar specs when building the new hiring app. No changes are needed to current screens.
