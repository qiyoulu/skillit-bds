## Brand Promise

**Scale your craft.**

For workers: access to construction's greatest opportunities.
For contractors: AI-first access to the skilled workforce they need.
For society: the capacity to build anything, anywhere.

_See `brand/narrative.md` — "The dream" and "The tagline" for the full context._

## Why Skillit Exists

The construction industry has spent decades assuming the problem is a labor shortage. The data says otherwise: only 1 in 20 workers are actually missing from the labor pool. The real constraint is a lack of **digital access** to the 95% of skilled workers who already exist.

_See `brand/narrative.md` — "The problem" for the detailed argument and key statistics._

## Competitive Positioning

**Competitors:** Traditional staffing agencies, job boards, workforce management platforms, emerging construction-tech startups (13+ in the U.S. alone).

**Skillit's difference:** Everyone else starts from the assumption of labor scarcity. Skillit starts from the insight that workers already exist — they just need to be found, understood, and connected.

**Category:** AI labor infrastructure for construction.

**Positioning statement:** Skillit is a worker-first network that uses AI to interpret live demand from America's largest contractors and intelligently route the right worker to the right project, place, and moment.

_See `brand/narrative.md` — "What Skillit is not" for the detailed exclusions._

## Brand Attributes

1. **Direct** — No hedging, no filler, no corporate speak. Words backed by data.
2. **Grounded** — Industrial functionalism. Materials from the jobsite. Real workers.
3. **Human** — Technology makes human skill more visible and essential.
4. **Craftsmanlike** — Sweat the details. Work you're proud of, even when no one is looking.
5. **Ambitious** — Solving one of the world's hardest, most stubborn problems.

_These map to the traits in `HUMAN-INSTRUCTIONS.md` and the design principles in `rules/layout.md`._

## Visual Direction (summary)

- **Essence:** Strong, useful, grounded. Industrial functionalism with humanity.
- **Color:** Traffic Cone as single accent. Iron for baseline. Canvas for ground. Plaster for surface.
- **Typography:** Work Sans for human content. JetBrains Mono for machine data. Sentence case.
- **Imagery:** Real workers, real job sites. Adobe Stock only, excluding generative AI. No decorative elements without purpose.
- **Shape language:** Pill buttons (actionable). 16px corners on static containers.
- **Motion:** CSS-only, reduced-motion accessible. No GIFs.

_See `tokens/docs/color.md`, `tokens/docs/typography.md`, `components/buttons.md` for the detailed rules._

## Brand Voice (summary)

- Active voice. Worker-first framing.
- No causation words ("is the reason why," "allows," "leads to").
- First-person executive voice is permitted in LinkedIn, PR, and investor contexts; use the channel register in `voice/brand-voice.md`.
- No claim-then-proof structure.
- No "but/however/actually."
- No villain framing.
- Numbers: digits, commas for ≥1,000.
- Percentages in digits.

_See `voice/brand-voice.md` for full voice rules and `voice/copy-examples.md` for examples._

## Success Metrics (12 months)

- Worker registrations and profile completion rates.
- Contractor retention and repeat usage.
- Brand consistency across all touchpoints (web, email, product, job site).
- Press mentions and share of voice in construction-tech.
- Team fluency in brand standards (every employee can name the five brand attributes).

## Reason to Believe

- **Founder's story:** Fraser Patterson was a journeyman carpenter and steel worker who struggled to find good work despite having valuable craft skills. Later, as a general contractor, he experienced the reverse. Same problem, two sides.
- **Data insight:** Only 1 in 20 workers are missing from the labor pool. The constraint is access, not scarcity.
- **Customer validation:** 13 of America's 25 largest datacenter builders trust Skillit.
- **Team traits:** Agency, judgment, craftsmanship, stamina, responsibility.

_See `brand/narrative.md` — "Founding credibility" and "Key statistics" for the full story and data._
